NVIDIA A800 SXM4 80 GB

The NVIDIA A800 SXM4 80GB GPU is a high-performance professional grade graphics processing unit designed to meet the demanding needs of modern data centers and computational workloads. With a base clock of 1155MHz and a boost clock of 1410MHz, this GPU delivers exceptional processing power and speed for a variety of applications. One of the standout features of the A800 is its massive 80GB of HBM2e memory, allowing for large datasets to be processed and stored with ease. The high memory clock speed of 1593MHz ensures that data can be accessed and utilized efficiently, resulting in improved overall performance. With a substantial 6912 shading units and 40MB of L2 cache, the A800 is capable of handling complex graphics and compute workloads with ease. Its impressive theoretical performance of 19.49 TFLOPS makes it well-suited for deep learning, AI, and scientific computing tasks. Despite its high-performance capabilities, the A800 does come with a hefty TDP of 400W, requiring adequate cooling and power delivery solutions to operate optimally. Overall, the NVIDIA A800 SXM4 80GB GPU is a powerhouse of a graphics processing unit, ideal for professionals and organizations that require top-tier performance for their data center and computational needs. Its high memory capacity, fast clock speeds, and robust shading units make it a valuable asset for a wide range of compute-intensive workloads.
