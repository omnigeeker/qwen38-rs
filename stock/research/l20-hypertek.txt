NVIDIA L20 48GB GPU
The NVIDIA L20 48GB GPU is a high-performance accelerator designed for AI inference and lightweight training workloads. Featuring 48GB of high-bandwidth memory, it delivers exceptional performance for large language models, computer vision, and recommendation systems while maintaining energy efficiency. Key advantages include superior memory capacity for handling complex AI models, optimized architecture for inference acceleration, and cost-effective deployment for enterprise AI applications. The L20 bridges the gap between consumer and data center GPUs, offering professional-grade AI capabilities with improved price-performance ratio, making it ideal for organizations seeking powerful AI processing without the premium cost of flagship data center solutions.

NVIDIA L20: Elevating Processing Power with Ada Lovelace Architecture
Embodying the cutting-edge Ada Lovelace architecture, the NVIDIA L20 stands as a technological marvel in the realm of GPUs. With 48 GB of GDDR6 memory featuring ECC and a formidable memory bandwidth of 864 GB/s, it ensures efficient data handling for demanding applications. The tensor cores, including INT8, FPB, BF16, and FP16, contribute to the L20’s robust processing capabilities, reaching up to 59.8 TFLOPS for FP32 operations. With support for RT cores, Multi-Instance GPU (MIG), and a substantial 96 MB L2 Cache, the L20 is designed for versatility and optimal workload management. Its power-efficient design, consuming 275 W and housed in a 2-slot FHFL form factor, positions the L20 as a dynamic solution for various computing needs.
